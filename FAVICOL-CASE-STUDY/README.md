# Fevicol — Consumer DIY Growth Strategy Case Study

> **From Professional Trust to Consumer Confidence**

An independent Business Strategy & Product Strategy case study exploring whether Fevicol can expand consumer-led DIY adoption while protecting the professional carpenter ecosystem that has contributed to its category leadership.

---

## 📌 Case Study Overview

**Company:** Pidilite Industries Limited  
**Brand:** Fevicol  
**Case Type:** Business Strategy / Product Strategy  
**Focus:** Consumer Growth, DIY, Customer Segmentation, Channel Strategy  
**Approach:** Independent Strategic Analysis  
**Status:** Conceptual Case Study

### Strategic Question

> **Should Fevicol continue protecting its established carpenter ecosystem, or launch a DIY proposition to acquire new consumers?**

The deeper business question is:

> **Will DIY create incremental growth — or simply shift existing professional demand to the consumer channel?**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Fevicol has built significant brand equity through its professional ecosystem, particularly among carpenters and woodworking professionals.

At the same time, consumer behaviour is evolving toward greater participation in simple home repairs, furniture fixes and DIY activities.

This creates a strategic dilemma:

### Option 01 — Protect the Core

Continue prioritising the established carpenter-led ecosystem.

**Potential benefit**
- Protect professional relationships
- Maintain existing demand
- Preserve channel trust

**Potential risk**
- Limited access to emerging consumer use cases
- Slower expansion into new consumption occasions

### Option 02 — Expand into DIY

Introduce a guided DIY proposition for selected low-complexity consumer jobs.

**Potential benefit**
- Acquire new consumers
- Create additional consumption occasions
- Expand consumer engagement with the brand

**Potential risk**
- Cannibalisation of professional-led demand
- Channel conflict
- Additional product, packaging and marketing investment
